❌⭕ About Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe, also known as Noughts and Crosses, is a free online classic strategy game from the Raxzen Game Collection.

Designed around the timeless 3×3 board game, this version combines simple and familiar gameplay with a premium tabletop-inspired interface, elegant visuals, smooth interactions, responsive controls, live scoring, and interactive sound effects.

The goal is simple: place your mark, create three in a row, and outsmart your opponent.

🎮 Two Ways to Play

Tic Tac Toe provides two simple game modes so you can play alone or with someone beside you.

🤖 Vs Computer
Challenge the built-in AI opponent. The computer checks for immediate winning opportunities, attempts to block your winning move when possible, and otherwise selects an available move. This creates a quick and accessible single-player experience.

👥 Vs Friend
Play locally with another person on the same device. Take turns placing X and O and see who can create the winning line first. No second device or account is required.

🧠 How to Play

  1. Choose Vs Computer or Vs Friend.
  2. X and O take turns automatically.
  3. Tap any empty square to place your mark.
  4. Create three matching marks in a row, column, or diagonal.
  5. The first player to complete a line wins.
  6. If every square is filled without a winning line, the round ends in a draw.

The game also highlights the winning line when a player wins.

🏆 Live Scoreboard

Tic Tac Toe includes a live scoreboard that keeps track of the current X and O scores during the session. After each round, players can choose to:

  • Play Again
  • Return to the Menu
  • Continue playing additional rounds

This makes it easy to turn a single match into a quick multi-round challenge.
