How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe Rules: Take turns placing X or O on a 3×3 grid. The first player to line up three matching marks in a row, column, or diagonal wins. If the board fills up with no line, the round ends in a draw.

Play Against the Computer: The built-in AI opponent looks for an available winning move first, then blocks your winning move when possible, and otherwise plays a legal move — a quick, accessible single-player match.

Play Tic Tac Toe With a Friend: Pass the same device back and forth and take turns placing X and O locally — no account or second device needed.

Tic Tac Toe on Mobile: The board and controls are touch-friendly and responsive, so the game plays comfortably on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops.

What Is Tic Tac Toe?

Tic Tac Toe, also known as Noughts and Crosses or the X and O game, is a classic two-player strategy puzzle played on a 3×3 grid. It's simple to learn, quick to play, and a timeless way to pass a few minutes solo or with a friend.

Tic Tac Toe Strategy Tips

  • Taking the center square first gives you the most possible winning lines.
  • Corners are generally stronger opening moves than edges.
  • Always check the board for your own winning move before doing anything else.
  • If you can't win this turn, check whether your opponent can — and block it.
  • Watch for "fork" opportunities, where one move creates two possible winning lines at once.

🧠 How to Play

  1. Choose Vs Computer or Vs Friend.
  2. X and O take turns automatically.
  3. Tap any empty square to place your mark.
  4. Create three matching marks in a row, column, or diagonal.
  5. The first player to complete a line wins.
  6. If every square is filled without a winning line, the round ends in a draw.

The game also highlights the winning line when a player wins.
