About Tic Tac Toe by Raxzen

Tic Tac Toe (tic-tac-toe.raxzen.in) is a fast, distraction-free Noughts and Crosses web game created by Raxzen. Play solo against the logic-based AI or challenge a friend on the same device.

Game Modes

  • Vs Computer: Smart AI checks for winning lines and blocks your threats.
  • Vs Friend: Classic two-player 3x3 match on one screen with live X/O scoring.
